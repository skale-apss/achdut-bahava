The Challenge

A divided people.
The cost is no longer tolerable.

It is a common understanding amongst Jews globally that we are a divided people — divisions that have caused detrimental impact to our economy, political system, national security, and our physical and spiritual wellbeing across generations.

🏛️
Political Instability

Internal divisions undermine unified national leadership and policy-making at every level.

🛡️
National Security

A fractured society is more vulnerable. As the saying goes — divide and conquer.

💰
Economic Cost

The cost is measurable in both human life and money. The alternative is no longer sustainable.

"As the saying goes: divide and conquer. To that, we say — no longer."

Purpose

Why We Exist

Vision

A unified Jewish world

Imagine what can be achieved in a world where global unity exists between all Jews — from across all its diverse spectrums — through active intention, mutual respect, and valued understanding of each other's diverse perspectives, so they can all co-exist and flourish without compromise.

Mission

100 cities. One commitment.

Establish weekly workshop meetups in 100 cities throughout Israel and the world, where Jews can come together to build and share safe bridges of respect, understanding, and love for each other's diverse perspectives.
