Our Commitment

The Meeting Promise

Read aloud at the opening of every workshop. These are not rules — they are our shared commitment to each other.

1

We meet each week with the shared commitment to listen deeply, seek to understand with empathic curiosity, and speak respectfully, before seeking to be understood.

2

We honour our Value Pillars, which are explained in our meeting rules guide, ensuring the unwavering emotional safety and wellbeing for all.

3

We start and end on time. No one speaks for more than three minutes without yielding the floor, and no one interrupts another speaker.

4

We distinguish facts from opinions, we challenge others through questions, and speak through personal experience only. Personal perspective is not fact.

5

We all agree to hold ourselves and each other accountable to these promises so that every voice can be heard, and progress toward mutual respect and understanding can be made.

The Journey

Achdut as a Journey

We have identified six stages people move through in relation to others' perspectives. Where are you on the journey?

1
Frustration

Where many begin — feeling misunderstood and unheard.

2
Understanding

Beginning to grasp why others hold their perspectives.

3
Appreciation

Recognising the validity and depth of different views.

4
Value

Actively valuing what diversity of thought brings.

5
Love

Genuine affinity and care for those who see the world differently.

6
Sharing

Becoming an ambassador — spreading unity beyond the workshop.

Hover over each stage to explore. The last two stages are highlighted as aspirational milestones.
